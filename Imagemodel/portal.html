RootRot-AI v2.1

Online System for Detecting and Assessing Dry Root Rot (DRR) in Chickpea

System Overview

Root Rot AI 2.1 is specialized for detecting Rhizoctonia bataticola (DRR) in chickpea images. The system utilizes two multitask transformer-based TensorFlow Lite models—one optimized for camera and root scanner modalities, and another for microscope images.

Technical Requirements

  • Red Background: Required for standard camera images.
  • Blue Background: Required for root scanner images.
  • White Background: Required for microscope images.
  • Image Integrity: Backgrounds must be clear of external objects; the model is specialized for plant images only.
  • Probabilistic Nature: All predictions are subject to a degree of error.
